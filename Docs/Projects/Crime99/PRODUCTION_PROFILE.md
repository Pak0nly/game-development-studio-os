## Product and Art Direction

- Crime99 is an elevated-camera urban action prototype with solo-testable Free-for-All, on-foot and vehicle combat, reactive city systems, short-form chaos, and the approved working direction `Neon over Dirt`.
- The Urban Slice Visual Assembly and Dressing evidence uses a mixed late-1980s/1990s neighbourhood commercial-corridor context. That context applies to the slice; it is not a universal final branding or lore decision.
- Human Developer approval remains required for final visual acceptance, new art direction, product/taste choices, brands, lore, scope, purchases, licence-risk acceptance, and production continuation.

## Crime99 Environment Composition Rules

Crime99 urban environments **MUST** derive visible composition from the
location's usage story, circulation, commerce, service, maintenance,
residential or industrial use, parking, loading, waste collection,
public/private boundaries, district identity, gameplay readability, and the
elevated camera. Props **MUST** follow qualified hosts and circulation; they
**MUST NOT** precede them.

Before visible environment creation or placement, the Task-Delta Work Order
**MUST** identify the relevant local asset families, inspect qualified
candidates, assess compatible modular combinations, distinguish Vendor
working copies from project-owned variants and wrappers, and justify every
visible placeholder. Research and inventory findings inform this decision but
do not qualify every asset family automatically.

Crime99 applies the following urban rules:

- **Sidewalks and curb zones:** preserve clear pedestrian movement,
  accessibility, storefront and residential entrances, crossings, vehicle
  access, loading interruptions, drainage logic, curb returns, and coherent
  road/pavement transitions. Curb geometry **MUST NOT** cross a valid entrance
  or duplicate a coplanar surface.
- **Storefronts:** awnings, signage structures, displays, seating, waste and
  delivery traces require real openings, business function, visibility,
  customer or staff use, and clear access. Decorative frontage props without
  those hosts **MUST NOT** be placed.
- **Alleys and service yards:** waste, pallets, loading equipment, utilities,
  gates and vehicles require a connected service, collection, maintenance,
  delivery, workshop, warehouse, transfer or access story. Public and service
  movement **MUST** remain distinguishable and usable.
- **Loading areas:** vehicle approach, stopping position, clearance, transfer
  point, door or storage relationship, and conflict with public circulation
  **MUST** be resolved before equipment is placed.
- **Utility pockets:** boxes, meters, ducts, vents, cables and protective
  elements **MUST** relate to a served building, wall, boundary or technical
  system and remain reachable for maintenance.
- **Parking and static vehicles:** each vehicle requires a credible parking,
  ownership, loading, service or temporary-stopping host, correct orientation,
  access and departure space. Vehicles **MUST NOT** be distributed as generic
  street decoration.
- **Gates and boundaries:** each gate requires a credible bounded access;
  each fence, wall or barrier requires security, ownership, construction,
  infrastructure, gameplay or continuation logic. Required entrances and
  routes remain open.
- **Elevated-camera readability:** hosts, routes, entrances, service/public
  distinction, landmarks, silhouettes and prop clusters **MUST** read from the
  representative elevated gameplay camera and relevant approach angles, not
  only from a favourable ground-level review.
- **Modular combinations:** compatible packages **MAY** be composed into
  distinct project-owned arrangements after provenance, scale, material,
  collider, camera and modular compatibility pass. Broad use requires
  representative proof of applicable straights, corners, endings, entrances,
  interruptions, transitions, closure and repetition.

The default disposition for a prop without a defensible Crime99 host, purpose,
orientation, clearance, and user/service/gameplay rationale is `REMOVE` or `DO
NOT PLACE`. Object-count or package-use targets cannot override this rule.

## Canonical Working Environments

- `Projects/Crime99` is the canonical technical gameplay repository. It
  contains ignored local manufacturer dependencies, and its 188 deliberately
  excluded status paths are protected from cleanup, reset, deletion, staging,
  or commit without a separate instruction.
- `Projects/Crime99MasterCityDiscovery` is the canonical Unity working
  environment for Crime99 city, world, and level production. This role does not
  mean that it is integrated with `Projects/Crime99`, that current production
  is authorised, or that an existing Gate-2/B11 scene is accepted. Because its
  URP/HDRP foundation choice is not globally resolved, every future authorised
  Unity task must name and verify the exact project root, pipeline, baseline,
  and allowed delta before execution.
- `Projects/Crime99AssetStylePreflight` is the evidence environment for the Urban Slice Visual Assembly and Dressing records. A Task-Delta Work Order must still identify the exact valid scene and dependency state before Unity use.
- Pilot worktrees are isolated evidence environments for their named pilots. They are not interchangeable production workspaces.
- Prefer a verified working environment above reconstructing another Unity project or transferred cache. No new complete project, full Vendor copy, or cache repair is authorised without explicit setup scope.

Changing the responsibility split between the canonical gameplay repository
and canonical city/world/level environment, or integrating them, requires a
separately authorised Human architecture or integration decision.

## Frozen Systems and Asset Boundaries

- Gate-D gameplay systems, camera policy, routes, vehicle/bot/combat systems, ProjectSettings, Packages, and manufacturer originals are frozen unless a Task-Delta Work Order explicitly includes them.
- Original Vendor archives, verified Vendor source folders outside an active
  Unity working project, and verified rollback sources remain local,
  protected, immutable, and restorable.
- Vendor files imported into an explicitly authorised Unity working project
  are technical working copies. Unity may reserialize, upgrade, reimport, or
  regenerate metadata for them when required by the authorised Unity version,
  dependency, package, or render-pipeline task. Hash drift alone is not a Hard
  Fail.
- Intentional visual or gameplay-facing changes, reusable compatibility work,
  and manual material/prefab tuning belong in traceable project-owned
  variants or overrides unless a Task-Delta Work Order explicitly authorises
  another reversible method.
- No purchase, download, import, installation, Vendor repair, licence interpretation, or full-package transfer is implied by an asset or production task.
- Evidence belongs outside Unity `Assets/` unless Unity import is required. Existing historical evidence under `Assets/` remains evidence; this rule does not authorise moving or deleting it.

## AI and Screenshot Boundary

- All Crime99 and Studio OS content is subject to the project-wide
  [Absolute No-Training and AI Service Boundary](../../AI_DEVELOPMENT_RULES.md#absolute-no-training-and-ai-service-boundary).
- Integrated Crime99-scene screenshots may be used for explicitly authorised,
  one-time current-project visual quality analysis only when the applicable
  provider plan, configuration, and terms support the required no-training
  boundary.
- Such screenshots may not be used for training, model improvement, dataset
  creation, style learning, imitation, reconstruction, or as generation
  references. They must not become a cross-project AI reference library.
- Raw or isolated Vendor assets remain subject to any stricter
  licence-specific restriction. Derived textual findings may be retained as
  Crime99 project feedback.
- Uncertain provider behaviour or licence scope stops submission for Human
  direction; Studio governance cannot override actual provider practices.

## Contextual Decision and Lesson Register

Task-Delta Work Orders load only IDs whose tags and scope match the task. Fields follow the [Contextual Decision and Lesson Record Template](../../Templates/CONTEXTUAL_DECISION_AND_LESSON_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md).

### C99-LSN-001 — Reduced closure is not the source asset

- **Tags:** `Diagnostic`, `Unity`, `closure`, `source-asset`
- **Trigger:** A reduced or transferred environment failed to compile or render representative content.
- **Verified cause:** Required dependencies or cache state were absent while the original source package remained viable.
- **Validated response:** Classify the reduced environment separately and validate dependency closure before source-family judgment.
- **Invalid shortcut:** Rejecting the source asset family from a reconstructed-environment failure.
- **Applies to:** transferred or reduced Unity environments and dependency diagnosis.
- **Does not apply to:** a representative failure reproduced in the verified source environment.
- **Current status:** Active.
- **Supersedes or successor:** Supersedes the unadopted broad family-rejection interpretation in Sprint 1; no successor.
- **Authority and evidence:** Urban Slice Visual Assembly final report, diagnostics 12.1, 12.11, and 12.13; Sprint 1 diagnostic reassess record.

### C99-LSN-002 — Representative render state before suitability judgment

- **Tags:** `Production`, `Diagnostic`, `visual`, `material-shader`
- **Trigger:** Grayscale, diagnostic, untextured, or incomplete-shader output was used for a final suitability inference.
- **Verified cause:** The evidence removed the authored material language required for the claim.
- **Validated response:** Use diagnostic output only for the questions it can prove; require representative materials, shader state, lighting conditions, distance, and camera for final visual or asset rejection.
- **Invalid shortcut:** Treating geometry-only evidence as material, period, style, or final visual evidence.
- **Applies to:** asset assembly, visual selection, and material/shader diagnosis.
- **Does not apply to:** explicitly bounded geometry, bounds, orientation, or proxy checks.
- **Current status:** Active.
- **Supersedes or successor:** None.
- **Authority and evidence:** Assembly final report diagnostics 12.2 and 12.8; Sprint 1 diagnostic reassess record.

### C99-LSN-003 — Orientation, bounds, and public frontage require proof

- **Tags:** `Production`, `Diagnostic`, `assembly`, `orientation`
- **Trigger:** Entrance, frontage, ground contact, or placement appeared wrong from root transforms or a single street view.
- **Verified cause:** Prefab pivots and local axes did not reliably describe the rendered public front or visible bounds.
- **Validated response:** Record rendered bounds, public-front convention, yaw, road face, entrances, overlap, sidewalk intrusion, and minimum Y.
- **Invalid shortcut:** Inferring visible fit from prefab root position or name.
- **Applies to:** building and prop assembly.
- **Does not apply to:** an already verified immutable placement unless the asset or transform changes.
- **Current status:** Active.
- **Supersedes or successor:** None.
- **Authority and evidence:** Assembly final report diagnostics 12.3 and 12.16; Sprint 1 orientation and reassess records.

### C99-LSN-004 — Replaced proxies remain a separate defect class

- **Tags:** `Production`, `Diagnostic`, `assembly`, `proxy`
- **Trigger:** Real content replaced blockout reservations but obsolete proxy state remained ambiguous.
- **Verified cause:** Renderer state and owning GameObject state were not both recorded.
- **Validated response:** Inventory named proxies, their owners and renderers, remove or explicitly retain them, and verify persistence after reload.
- **Invalid shortcut:** Calling a replacement complete because one renderer was disabled.
- **Applies to:** blockout-to-asset replacement and scene cleanup.
- **Does not apply to:** intentional project-owned proxies still required by the accepted stage.
- **Current status:** Active.
- **Supersedes or successor:** None.
- **Authority and evidence:** Assembly final report diagnostic 12.4; Sprint 1 diagnostic reassess record.

### C99-LSN-005 — Causal defect classes precede FAIL

- **Tags:** `Diagnostic`, `review`, `causal-fail`
- **Trigger:** Package, selection, assembly, orientation, proxy, material, closure, composition, camera, blockout, and accepted-concern findings were conflated.
- **Verified cause:** A visible symptom was treated as one undifferentiated asset-family failure.
- **Validated response:** Record symptom, verified cause, representative-evidence status, scope, and falsifying evidence before disposition.
- **Invalid shortcut:** Issuing a broad rejection from incomplete, diagnostic, or wrong-camera evidence.
- **Applies to:** causal diagnosis and negative asset/visual verdicts.
- **Does not apply to:** a directly observed representative hard fail with a verified cause.
- **Current status:** Active.
- **Supersedes or successor:** Supersedes Sol #8's unadopted broad family rejection; later diagnostics are the successor evidence.
- **Authority and evidence:** Assembly final report diagnostics 12.7, 12.8, and 12.10; Sprint 1 reassess and Human supersession records.

### C99-LSN-006 — Camera limits and blockout limits are not spatial invalidity

- **Tags:** `Diagnostic`, `visual`, `camera-evidence`, `blockout`
- **Trigger:** A documentary camera, open horizon, missing dressing, or formative finish was used to reject spatial composition.
- **Verified cause:** Evidence and stage questions were broader than the candidate was authorised to answer.
- **Validated response:** State each camera and stage claim; defer runtime-only, dressing, and final-art claims to their applicable stage.
- **Invalid shortcut:** Treating limited evidence or authorised blockout incompleteness as a structural failure.
- **Applies to:** blockout, formative assembly, and bounded documentary capture.
- **Does not apply to:** a primary success condition that explicitly requires gameplay-scale presentation.
- **Current status:** Active.
- **Supersedes or successor:** None.
- **Authority and evidence:** Assembly final report diagnostics 12.5 and 12.6.

### C99-LSN-007 — Prefer a verified working environment

- **Tags:** `Diagnostic`, `Integration`, `Unity`, `environment`
- **Trigger:** Repeated repair of copied cache or incomplete project state continued after a working source environment was known.
- **Verified cause:** Environment reconstruction was mistaken for required production work.
- **Validated response:** With explicit authority, use the verified working environment, isolate project-owned outputs, and retain the failed copy as diagnostic evidence.
- **Invalid shortcut:** Creating another full project or Vendor copy to avoid verifying the canonical environment.
- **Applies to:** Unity recovery and asset integration.
- **Does not apply to:** an explicitly authorised clean-room or migration test.
- **Current status:** Active.
- **Supersedes or successor:** None.
- **Authority and evidence:** Assembly final report diagnostics 12.11–12.15.

### C99-LSN-008 — Capture validity is a gate

- **Tags:** `Diagnostic`, `Production`, `visual`, `capture-evidence`
- **Trigger:** Bounded capture runs produced identical background-only images despite reaching the capture step.
- **Verified cause:** The local URP capture path failed; a completed batch invocation did not prove usable pixels.
- **Validated response:** Check distinctness, foreground, expected content, fallback colours, candidate identity, and renderer health before accepting evidence.
- **Invalid shortcut:** Consuming a creative correction or issuing a visual verdict from an invalid capture run.
- **Applies to:** Unity raster evidence and formal visual review readiness.
- **Does not apply to:** a valid capture whose representative content fails the visual criteria.
- **Current status:** Active.
- **Supersedes or successor:** Reclassifies the historical Dressing `1/1 consumed` capture failure under the stage-local semantics adopted through LAIA-2026-07-22. The adopted accounting governs future separately authorised work and is not retroactive.
- **Authority and evidence:** Dressing final report sections 16 and Diagnostic Lessons; formal correction technical failure record.

### C99-LSN-009 — Rollback requires candidate identity proof

- **Tags:** `Diagnostic`, `Integration`, `Unity`, `rollback`
- **Trigger:** Named scene objects and labels were logically restored but the scene hash changed.
- **Verified cause:** Unity serialization state was not reproduced byte-identically outside Unity.
- **Validated response:** Restore a recoverable byte-identical snapshot or treat the scene as a new candidate requiring full affected validation.
- **Invalid shortcut:** Calling a scene restored because its intended objects appear equivalent.
- **Applies to:** Unity scene correction transactions and rollback.
- **Does not apply to:** a deliberately new candidate with fresh complete validation.
- **Current status:** Active.
- **Supersedes or successor:** The validated `C2A018...` checkpoint remains prior evidence; `977CDE...` is not its successor.
- **Authority and evidence:** Dressing final report section 16 and Diagnostic Lessons; formal correction technical failure record.

### C99-LSN-010 — Required clusters need schema enforcement

- **Tags:** `Production`, `visual`, `dressing`, `schema`
- **Trigger:** Validation text claimed five functional clusters while placement records contained only A-D.
- **Verified cause:** Free-text validation was not cross-checked against manifest cardinality and retained-object evidence.
- **Validated response:** Validate required IDs, nonzero retained records, and candidate evidence directly from the manifest.
- **Invalid shortcut:** Treating an existing bench or nearby prop as an unnamed substitute for missing Cluster E.
- **Applies to:** Urban Slice Dressing cluster requirements and similar schema-backed placement tasks.
- **Does not apply to:** tasks with no declared required-cluster contract.
- **Current status:** Active.
- **Supersedes or successor:** None.
- **Authority and evidence:** Dressing final report sections 5, 16, and Diagnostic Lessons.

### C99-LSN-011 — Primary-camera success cannot be delegated to detail views

- **Tags:** `Production`, `visual`, `camera-evidence`, `dressing`
- **Trigger:** Service and landmark construction read in dedicated views but not at gameplay scale.
- **Verified cause:** Improvements were localised outside the primary camera's useful visual hierarchy.
- **Validated response:** Bind every player-facing success condition to a named primary gameplay capture and use detail views only as support.
- **Invalid shortcut:** Passing a player-facing objective from beauty or detail evidence alone.
- **Applies to:** production tasks whose objective is gameplay-scale readability.
- **Does not apply to:** a task explicitly limited to hidden construction or a diagnostic detail claim.
- **Current status:** Active.
- **Supersedes or successor:** None.
- **Authority and evidence:** Dressing final report sections 7-11 and Diagnostic Lessons.

### C99-LSN-012 — Preflight evidence before a formal correction transaction

- **Tags:** `Production`, `Diagnostic`, `visual`, `correction`
- **Trigger:** Mutation and formal correction capture were coupled before renderer health was re-established.
- **Verified cause:** The evidence path failed, leaving no valid corrected candidate and a non-identical rollback.
- **Validated response:** Run a non-mutating capture-health preflight against the authoritative scene, preserve a recoverable checkpoint, then mutate and freeze transactionally.
- **Invalid shortcut:** Spending a stage correction before the evidence path can produce a reviewable candidate.
- **Applies to:** visual corrections after a known capture or serialization failure.
- **Does not apply to:** formative work that does not claim a formal candidate or verdict.
- **Current status:** Active.
- **Supersedes or successor:** Successor to the historical Dressing correction procedure for future separately authorised work.
- **Authority and evidence:** Dressing final report sections 16 and 22 and Diagnostic Lessons; formal correction technical failure record.

### C99-LSN-013 — Imported working-copy hash drift is not source corruption

- **Tags:** `Integration`, `Unity`, `provenance`, `pipeline-migration`
- **Trigger:** An authorised Unity/HDRP import reserialized imported Vendor
  working-copy materials while original archives and verified rollback
  sources remained exact and restorable.
- **Verified cause:** Unity technically adapted serialized working copies
  during pipeline activation/import; byte identity was used as the stop test
  without first testing semantic or functional loss.
- **Validated response:** Preserve original and rollback sources, inventory
  the working-copy diff, then verify additions/removals, GUIDs, references,
  material/shader usability, relevant scene opening/rendering, package roots,
  and recovery. Treat explained reserialization as a Concern unless it causes
  a functional, provenance, or recovery failure.
- **Invalid shortcut:** Calling any working-copy hash drift a Hard Fail, or
  accepting unexplained destructive drift merely because Unity performed it.
- **Applies to:** Explicitly authorised Unity import, version, dependency, and
  render-pipeline work on imported Vendor working copies.
- **Does not apply to:** Original Vendor archives, protected source folders
  outside the active project, verified rollback sources, unauthorised manual
  edits, deletion, reference loss, or no-training violations.
- **Current status:** Active.
- **Supersedes or successor:** Supersedes the future-use byte-identity stop
  rule in `C99-HDRP-WAREHOUSE-MIGRATION-V1`; historical evidence and rollback
  outcome remain traceable.
- **Authority and evidence:** DEC-024; governance revision
  `SOS-GOV-WCM-NTB-V1`; `C99-HDRP-WAREHOUSE-MIGRATION-V1` execution evidence.
