## Successful Applications

No current Military Base subset is adopted. The exact wired-fence straight,
corner, end-cap and double-gate subset is now visually qualified as a usable
composition option with a project-owned alpha-clipped material. Its static
family remains a strong alternative to bespoke perimeter and industrial-support
modelling.

## Failed Applications

Earlier pipeline/gate-lock concern means the family cannot be assumed ready in
the canonical URP scene. This is a configuration/subset issue, not proof that
fences, gates or industrial props are unsuitable.

The first 2026-08-26 qualification candidate used one neutral opaque override
for every material slot. Direct inspection showed solid rectangular plates
instead of chain-link and an unusable player camera inside a building. The
formal correction fixed those exact defects; neither result generalises to the
whole asset family.

## Decision Guidance

- Prefer for a named perimeter, storage or security-infrastructure gap.
- Compare with NYC and Port holdings before adding a new visual family.
- Use project-owned variants and restrained Crime99 material treatment.
- Compose final runs against the real parcel line and inspect gate circulation,
  corners and wall/fence transitions in context. Small post-hidden overlaps are
  acceptable when they are not visible to the player.
- Keep Industrial Harbour modular brick walls available as interchangeable
  solid-screening or mixed-boundary options; this qualification neither selects
  nor rejects them.
- Technical debt: moderate pipeline/style harmonisation; high if full scenes are imported.
