## Earliest Invalid Gate and Consequences

Phase 4 is the earliest invalid gate. Its accepted record claimed representative traversal and camera/occlusion proof, but the final semantic audit established that:

- the labelled vehicle route teleported the player and changed only a camera offset;
- the foot test moved only `1.5 m` and did not traverse the required route;
- occlusion frames reset independently and therefore did not prove Entry, Behind, Exit, Restored, Re-entry, flicker resistance, permanent-hide resistance, or target switching; and
- the occlusion assertion accepted any non-negative renderer count rather than checking the required state change and outcome.

Phase 4 is therefore `REOPENED / FAIL`. Phase-5 through Phase-8 passages are invalidated. Their artifacts remain historical evidence only and may not be read as valid gate passage or a production base.

- A hidden dependency on the old Gley demo slice was detected at Phase 4 before licensed environment-asset integration.

- The final Human boundary was reached without unauthorised continuation.
- Candidate hashes, failed executions, final role verdicts, and late invalidation were retained rather than concealed.
