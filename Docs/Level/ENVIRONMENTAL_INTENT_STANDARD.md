# Environmental Intent Standard

## Purpose

This standard defines the minimum intent record required before functional planning or layout begins. It prevents a level area from becoming an arrangement of assets without a use, gameplay purpose, or visual thesis.

Realism is supporting evidence, not the product goal. A project may simplify, exaggerate, relocate, compress, or omit real-world features for readability, action, navigation, accessibility, camera behaviour, performance, or fun when the benefit and resulting risks are explicit.

## Required Rules

- Complete one record for each materially distinct level area or reusable zone type.
- Distinguish confirmed project direction, observed reference, inference, and project adaptation.
- Keep answers short and testable; link detailed references instead of copying them.
- Record structural changes in a [Level Decision Record](../Templates/LEVEL_DECISION_RECORD_TEMPLATE.md).
- If the intent cannot explain the area's use and gameplay purpose, stop before functional planning.
- The Level Designer and Art Director review the record from their domains. Activate the Narrative / Worldbuilding Reviewer when it contains material world, period, culture, or story claims.

## Pass and Fail

**Pass:** every required question is answered; use, gameplay, mood, and deviations agree; claims are traceable; unresolved items do not block planning.

**Fail:** the place exists only as decoration; primary use or gameplay function is missing; deviations are unexplained; approved direction is contradicted; or a material claim is presented as fact without evidence or approval.
