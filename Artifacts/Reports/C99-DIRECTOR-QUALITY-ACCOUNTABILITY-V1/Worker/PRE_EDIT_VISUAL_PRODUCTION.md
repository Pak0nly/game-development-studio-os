# Visual Production Playbook

> **Status:** Adopted through LAIA-2026-07-22 on 2026-07-22; amended through
> `SOS-GOV-WCM-NTB-V1` on 2026-07-25 and `SOS-GOV-AAPEC-V1` on 2026-07-29.
> This playbook does not itself grant execution permission.

## Use

Load this playbook for bounded visible production on an accepted baseline or a separately Human-authorised correction of one preserved failed-stage candidate. New or structurally material level work uses the [Level Development Lifecycle](../Level/LEVEL_DEVELOPMENT_LIFECYCLE.md) instead of treating this playbook as a shortcut around structural gates.

## Studio-wide Environment Composition Rule

Work **MUST** know and qualify the available asset vocabulary before creating
or placing visible replacements. Environments **MUST** be composed from
spatial purpose, real or project usage, gameplay needs, and approved
art-direction intent. Every visible element requires a defensible purpose, and
every prop requires a credible host. Qualified existing assets and modular
combinations **MUST** be considered before new visible placeholder geometry.

## Required Inputs

- exact accepted baseline or preserved Human-authorised failed-stage candidate, plus the project Production Profile;
- assignment-specific visual objective and frozen systems;
- approved art direction and representative gameplay camera;
- location function, usage story, circulation, entrances, service and
  maintenance access, and the valid host zones affected by the task;
- the relevant qualified asset vocabulary, modular proof state, Vendor versus
  project-owned boundary, and any controlled placeholder declaration;
- relevant contextual lesson IDs;
- named stage-exit review and correction allowance; and
- applicable Visual QA claims, hard fails, and evidence locations.

## Workflow

1. Verify the baseline, protected sources, and allowed visible delta.
2. Define the location function, usage story, circulation, access, spatial
   hierarchy, and primary player-facing claim before editing. Supporting
   beauty or detail views cannot replace it.
3. Inspect and qualify the relevant asset vocabulary before creating visible
   replacements. Reuse and coherent non-destructive composition **MUST** be
   considered before bespoke visible invention.
4. Establish buildings, boundaries, functional zones, and valid prop hosts
   before props. Every retained or new visible element **MUST** have a
   defensible gameplay, world, art-direction, or composition purpose and a
   credible host.
5. Place props from actual use, with orientation and clearance that preserve
   pedestrian, vehicle, entrance, loading, maintenance, accessibility, and
   camera needs. Hostless or space-filling distribution **MUST NOT** proceed.
6. Resolve materials and surface transitions only after composition and
   functional placement pass. Decals, wear, dirt, ageing, lighting, and
   atmosphere **MUST NOT** conceal structural or compositional defects.
7. Work formatively without freezing a final evidence package after each small
   edit. Use representative materials, lighting, distance, and camera before
   rejecting visible content or an asset family.
8. Preflight the evidence path non-mutatively when a prior capture, renderer,
   shader, or pipeline failure is relevant.
9. Prepare one exact candidate that satisfies the
   [Definition of Ready](../Production/EXECUTION_MODES_AND_STAGE_REVIEW_STANDARD.md#definition-of-ready-for-formal-review).
10. Freeze current evidence and run one meaningful stage-exit review under the
    [Visual Quality Assurance Workflow](../Visual/Visual_Quality_Assurance_Workflow.md).
11. Apply only the declared stage-local formal corrections, regenerate
    affected evidence, and rerun affected reviewers.

## Review Selection

Activate only roles whose distinct visual, spatial, technical-art, provenance, camera, gameplay-readability, evidence, or scope finding can change the stage decision. Use the full Visual Level Review Board only when the work is genuinely cross-domain, structurally material, or its risk makes every board domain decision-relevant.

## AI Screenshot Analysis Boundary

Integrated project-scene screenshots may be supplied for explicitly
authorised one-time current-project visual quality analysis only when the
[Absolute No-Training and AI Service Boundary](../AI_DEVELOPMENT_RULES.md#absolute-no-training-and-ai-service-boundary)
is verified for the applicable provider plan, configuration, and terms.

Do not use screenshots for training, model improvement, dataset creation,
style learning, imitation, reconstruction, or as generation references. Do
not create a persistent cross-project image/reference library. Raw or isolated
Vendor textures, meshes, materials, prefabs, or previews remain subject to
stricter licence-specific restrictions. Derived findings may be stored as
project feedback.

When provider behaviour or the content licence is uncertain, withhold the
content, record the uncertainty, and require Human approval.

## Stop

Stop before further mutation for invalid candidate identity,
nonrepresentative evidence that cannot be repaired within the diagnostic
allowance, an earlier structural defect, missing asset-vocabulary
qualification, an unproven modular requirement, absent prop hosts or
circulation, an unjustified visible placeholder, exhausted stage-local
corrections, a visible hard fail outside scope, or any need for new art
direction, scope, architecture, asset, licence, purchase, download,
protected-source, or Git authority. Complete the factual record even when
mutation stops.
