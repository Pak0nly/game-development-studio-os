## Verdict against the balance target

- Highest average duel win rate over all configurations: shield with 63.8 % (target: none above 60 %): NOT MET.
- Highest duel win rate inside one single bot configuration: hoop (circler) with 84.7 %: above 60 %, so the target is not met for every play style.
- Stricter reading, mean of the rusher-only and circler-only duel rates (the mixed duels are left out because there the bot style decides, not the drawing): shield with 68.4 %: above 60 %.
- Spread of the duel win rates round 50 % (root mean square): 10.2 points.
- Archetypes at or above 50 %: ring, line, comb, shield, hedgehog, needle, spiral, grille, splinter; shape families among them: hollow, blades, core+blades, solid, small (target: at least three clearly different shapes): MET.
- Highest free-for-all win rate: block with 42.0 % (even would be 25 %).
- Light shapes (at most 70 cells: cross, line, dot, needle, splinter) in four-figure rounds: average 15.1 %; at or above 12.5 % (half of even): cross, dot, needle, splinter. Worker's own yardstick "not hopeless" (average at least 12.5 % and at least two light shapes at 12.5 %): MET.

## What this simulation can and cannot tell

- It can tell which drawings win under two simple play styles: the rusher runs at the nearest opponent and dashes by chance; the circler picks an opponent outside the crowd, circles it and rams when its dash is ready and its front points at the target. A drawing that is strong under both is strong for a structural reason; one that is strong under only one is partly a bot artefact.
- The mixed configuration says little about drawings: when a rusher meets a circler the rusher wins about nine duels in ten whatever is drawn (`node sim/style_check.js`: 91.7 % of 2448 duels with the final tuning). Its duel column is therefore close to 50 % for every drawing and pulls the "all" average toward 50 %. Read the rusher and circler columns for the effect of the drawing.
- It cannot tell how the game feels, and it cannot find tricks. Bots never dodge, bait, wait or gang up on purpose; they do not redraw between rounds and do not use catch-up ink. Two styles are still only two styles.
- Many pairings are close to 0 or 100 percent because both bots repeat the same behaviour; the seeds only change start sides, wobble and dash timing. Read the averages as a ranking, not as odds between humans.
- The archetype field is hand-made. Four "player optimiser" drawings were added to look for rule-breaking designs, but the numbers were still chosen while looking at this field.
