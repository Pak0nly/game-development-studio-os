## What this simulation can and cannot tell

- It can tell which drawings win under two simple play styles: the rusher runs at the nearest opponent and dashes by chance; the circler picks an opponent outside the crowd, circles it and rams when its dash is ready and its front points at the target. A drawing that is strong under both is strong for a structural reason; one that is strong under only one is partly a bot artefact.
- The mixed configuration says little about drawings: when a rusher meets a circler the rusher wins about four duels in five whatever is drawn (`node sim/style_check.js`: 79.3 % of 1520 duels with the final tuning). Its duel column is therefore close to 50 % for every drawing and pulls the "all" average toward 50 %. Read the rusher and circler columns for the effect of the drawing.
- It cannot tell how the game feels, and it cannot find tricks. Bots never dodge, bait, wait or gang up on purpose; they do not redraw between rounds and do not use catch-up ink. Two styles are still only two styles.
- Many pairings are close to 0 or 100 percent because both bots repeat the same behaviour; the seeds only change start sides, wobble and dash timing. Read the averages as a ranking, not as odds between humans.
- The archetype field is hand-made. Six "player optimiser" drawings were added to look for rule-breaking designs, but the numbers were still chosen while looking at this field.
