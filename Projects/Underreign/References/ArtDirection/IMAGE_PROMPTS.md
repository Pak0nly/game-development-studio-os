# Underreign — Image Prompts for a Rough Look of the Peoples (ChatGPT)

Status: exploration only. Images made from these prompts are rough references
for the Human, not approved art. If a result is kept in the repository, store
it under `Projects/Underreign/References/ArtDirection/` with a provenance note
(tool, date, prompt) and respect DEC-025.

Paste the shared style block first, then one people block. One image per
people.

## Shared style block

Stylised 3D game character concept art for an original underground fantasy
strategy game. Clean, readable shapes, clear saturated colours, soft
directional light, dark fantasy with a mischievous touch. Full-body line-up on a
dark neutral background: one leader, one worker, two creatures, with a small
vignette of their underground architecture behind them. Original designs, not
based on or resembling any existing game, film or franchise. No text, no logos.

## 1. Ashen Pact (fire and contracts)

A people of tall, lean infernal beings whose skin is a black, cracked crust like
cooling lava, with orange glow in the seams. Curved horns made of black volcanic
glass with embers glowing inside; each horn carries thin brass rings, one for
every contract they have sealed. Sparse clothing: brass chains, wax-sealed
contract scrolls hanging from the belt, red wax seals pressed into the skin as
rank marks, eyes like glowing coals. Show a proud contract-keeper as leader, a
hound-like fire beast, a witch-like ember caster, and a knee-high soot worker
with a glowing ember in its chest that leaves ash footprints. Architecture:
black basalt hall with brass fittings, doors sealed with red wax, walls carved
with glowing contract script. Palette: black, ember orange, brass, wax red.

## 2. Mycelid Choir (fungi, one voice)

A fungal people made of stacked mushroom caps and stalks, from hunched to
towering, with softly glowing gills under the caps and fine white threads
trailing behind them into the ground; they seem to hum in chorus. Show a
towering spore-matron as leader, a squat warden covered in shelf fungus, a
puffball creature, and a small round puffball worker on root-like feet.
Architecture: caves overgrown with white mycelium mats, cap-shaped roofs, spore
haze, bioluminescent blue-green light. Palette: bone white, moss green,
bioluminescent teal, rot brown.

## 3. Drowned Court (eerie deep-sea people)

An uncanny but dignified court from the eternal darkness of the deep sea. Almost
translucent pale skin showing glowing veins, an anglerfish-like glowing lure
hanging from the forehead, too many small dark eyes, long thin limbs. They wear
salt-crusted court robes that never dry, coral crowns and mother-of-pearl
jewellery; water always drips from them. Show a noble oracle as leader, an
eel-like knight, a crab-like brute in wreck plating, and a small
hermit-crab-like servant in shell armour as worker. Architecture: a sunken
palace of barnacled marble columns, shell mosaics, dark pools and small
waterfalls between floors. Palette: deep teal, pearl white, bioluminescent cyan,
coral pink.

## 4. Gearbound Kin (kobold tinkerers)

Small, wiry kobolds (folklore kobolds: mammal-like, not reptilian) with very
large expressive ears, big curious eyes and quick hands, clever and fussy. Soot-
stained leather aprons, magnifying goggles, many tool belts, everything they own
patched together from scrap. Show a chief tinkerer with a mechanical arm as
leader, a small steam-driven golem, a rickety gyrocopter scout, and a worker
that is a walking wooden box on four spider-like metal legs with a ticking
wind-up spring on top. Architecture: a workshop cave full of pipes, gears,
steam vents, chain lifts with counterweights and trapdoors; brass and oak,
yellow-black warning stripes. Palette: copper, oak brown, soot black, warning
yellow.

## 5. Deep Dwarves (proposal, pending the Human's round-4 answer)

Dwarves who never left the depths for centuries, clearly different from
surface dwarves: pale skin, large light-sensitive eyes, beards braided with
stone beads and ore rings, armour of living rock that slowly grows onto their
bodies. Grim, patient, unbreakable. Show a stone-armoured warden as leader, a
burrowing beast they ride, a heavy wall-builder, and a young digger as worker.
Architecture: massive fortress halls with reinforced floors and ceilings,
layered stone vaults, sealed shafts. Palette: slate grey, dark granite, ore
green, dim amber lamplight.

## 6. Crystal Brood (later)

An insect-like hive people with translucent crystal carapaces that break light
into colours; they communicate with ringing tones. Show a queen-like singer as
leader, a warrior with crystal blades, a flying scout, and a small worker
carrying crystal shards. Architecture: hives of hexagonal crystal cells glowing
from within. Palette: clear crystal, violet, amber, cold white light.
